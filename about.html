🛠️ About This Project

Lift Maintenance Management System Using Python

📖 Our Story

This project is a fully functional Python-based desktop application developed to automate lift maintenance management in buildings, offices, hospitals, and commercial complexes. It centralizes lift records, service schedules, technician assignments, repair history, and complaint handling.

Our work focuses on creating a practical, reliable, and professional system that improves maintenance planning, safety, and reporting efficiency.

🎯 Our Mission

To build a dependable and efficient lift maintenance system that uses Python and modern software design principles to solve real-world maintenance challenges.

  • ✅ Automate lift maintenance operations
  • ✅ Store lift information securely
  • ✅ Schedule preventive maintenance
  • ✅ Generate accurate maintenance reports
  • ✅ Improve lift safety and reliability
